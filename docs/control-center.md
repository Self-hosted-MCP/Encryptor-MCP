# The control center

Your own page for your vaults. Your AI never sees it.

## Opening it

- **On a computer:** ask your AI app "Open my Encryptor control center": it opens in your browser, signed in. Or open it yourself and unlock any vault with its password: that signs you in too. The page of a vault you just unlocked has an "All vaults" link as well.
- **On a server:** open your server's address and sign in with your passphrase. You can let the browser remember you for 90 days.

## Vaults

Each vault shows whether it's open, which app it's lent to and when it's due back. Unlock, Lock, Lock all, Open, Settings and Add a vault are all here.

## A vault's page

- **Due back:** when it locks at the latest, and after how long without use.
- **Browse files:** see folders, open pictures and text, download anything. Decrypted only in memory.
- **Show in Finder** (Mac): the open vault as a drive.
- **Record:** every change, with who made it and why, and an Undo button.
- **Trash:** restore things, or delete the trash for good (asked twice).
- **Read log:** which files were opened, by which app and why. Private to you, checked for tampering.
- **Health:** checks the vault is whole; repairs what can be repaired safely, after asking.

## Settings

- **Vaults:** each with its own page: what the AI may do (read and change, read only, hidden from AI), default vault, its own auto-lock time, rename, change folder, remove from the list. Critical changes ask twice.
- **Add a vault** or **create a new vault** (with its recovery key, shown once).
- **Password and recovery key** (on a vault's page): change the password, show the recovery key, or set a new password with it.
- **Auto-lock** for all vaults.
- **Safety:** read only for every AI app; whether AI apps may ask to delete for good.
- On a server: **connected AI apps**, with which vaults each may ask for, and Disconnect.

## Activity and About

- **Activity:** signed-in browsers (sign out one or all others) and the sign-ins of the last 7 days. Private to you.
- **About:** version, an optional daily update check, license and help links.

Light by default, with a Dark switch at the top right. Works on a phone.
